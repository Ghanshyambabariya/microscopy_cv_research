## Notes For Discussion

- The project separates the different research directions instead of mixing everything into one notebook.
- Real benchmark data is used where practical, especially for tool-wear and concrete-strength experiments.
- Synthetic and simulated data are labeled as development scaffolding.
- The next major improvement is stronger microscopy datasets plus pretrained encoder comparisons.
